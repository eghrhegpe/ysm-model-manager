# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct（贡献者行为准则）

## Our Pledge（我们的承诺）

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

为了营造开放、友好的环境，我们作为贡献者与维护者承诺：无论年龄、体型、残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何，任何人在本项目中都不会受到骚扰。

## Our Standards（行为标准）

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language（使用友好、包容的语言）
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints（尊重不同观点）
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism（大方接受建设性批评）
- Focusing on what is best for the community（以社区利益为先）
- Showing empathy towards other community members（对社区成员抱有同理心）

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery（使用性暗示的语言或图像）
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments（挑衅、侮辱、贬低性言论）
- Public or private harassment（公开或私下的骚扰）
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission（未经许可公开他人隐私信息）
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate（其他可合理认为不当的行为）

## Responsibilities（维护者职责）

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

## Scope（适用范围）

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, discussions, and any other communication channels), and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement（执行）

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/eghrhegpe/ysm-model-manager/issues) or private channels. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may be removed from the project team.

基于本项目的单人维护模式，投诉由仓库所有者统一受理与裁决。
